Privacy Policy

Application: Keuangan-CBL / Financial SaaS (Pilot Release) | Operator: Muhammad Fikri
Last updated: September 2026

1. Overview and Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Muhammad Fikri ("operator", "we", "us", or "our"), as the individual operator responsible for the Keuangan-CBL / Financial SaaS pilot platform, handles information collected through the application, web user interfaces, and connected messaging intake channels, including Meta WhatsApp integrations.

The platform provides financial workflow support and document intake for participating pilot organizations. This policy covers data collected and processed through our web portal and supported messaging channels.

2. Information We Process

We process only information required to provide document intake, financial candidate staging, and accounting workflows:

  • User and Account Information: Name, professional email address, credentials/authentication tokens, organizational role, and tenant organization association.
  • WhatsApp Sender Details & Transport Metadata: Sender phone number, WhatsApp message identifiers, message direction, delivery status, and timestamps.
  • WhatsApp Messages: Text messages, photo captions, and clarification responses sent to connected intake channels.
  • Uploaded Images and PDF Documents: Image files (such as JPEG, PNG, WEBP, HEIC) and PDF files submitted via web upload or WhatsApp intake (including receipts, vendor invoices, customer billing documents, payment slips, and project documentation).
  • Extracted Document Data: Extracted document fields, such as billing parties, dates, line items, monetary amounts, reference numbers, and project codes.
  • Financial Transaction Candidates: Structured transaction proposals derived from document extraction and awaiting human review.
  • Audit Records: Records capturing user/actor actions, entity identifiers, timestamps, and state changes to ensure operational traceability.

3. Purposes of Processing

Information is processed for the following operational purposes:

  1. Document Intake & Ingestion: Ingesting operational documents submitted by authorized personnel via web upload or messaging integrations.
  2. Document Data Extraction: Analyzing uploaded images and PDFs via OCR and automated extraction to identify relevant transaction fields.
  3. Transaction Candidate Preparation: Generating draft financial proposals based on extracted document information.
  4. Human Verification via Review Queue: Staging proposed candidates for verification, correction, or rejection by authorized organization operators.
  5. Accounting Execution: Processing approved candidates through the core accounting engine into double-entry accounting records.
  6. Audit Logging & Operational Integrity: Maintaining traceability of document submissions, reviews, approvals, and transaction history.

4. Document Processing, AI/OCR Extraction, and the Review Queue

The platform uses optical character recognition (OCR) and automated extraction technology (which may include local heuristics or external vision/AI providers) to parse information from uploaded images and PDF files.

Core Processing Principle: Automated OCR and AI extraction outputs are solely draft proposals. Inbound WhatsApp messages and raw document uploads do not directly create accounting journals or modify general ledger balances.

All parsed documents and proposed transactions must pass through a Review Queue. An authorized human reviewer within the organization must verify the document contents, validate or adjust the candidate information, and explicitly approve the transaction before any financial records or journals are created. Inbound transfer proofs or receipts alone do not automatically constitute financial expenses.

5. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

We do not sell personal or financial data. Information is shared only with third-party service providers as needed to operate the system:

  • Meta Cloud API (WhatsApp): For messaging channel intake, message routing and media downloads occur via Meta Platforms infrastructure pursuant to Meta platform terms.
  • Document Extraction / Vision Services: Where configured, document images or PDF pages may be transmitted to external document extraction or vision processing services to extract textual and financial information.
  • Hosting and Infrastructure: Application servers, databases, and file storage environments used to host platform services.

6. Security Controls

We implement operational and technical security measures appropriate for the service, including:

  • Access Control: Role-based access controls and authenticated user session validation.
  • Tenant Isolation: Logical isolation of data between distinct tenant organizations.
  • Integrity Checks: Cryptographic file hashing to detect duplicate uploads and verify file consistency.
  • Webhook Authentication: Inbound webhook signature verification and transport handshake controls.
  • Audit Logging: Append-only audit records of critical user actions, corrections, and approvals.

7. Data Retention

Data is retained as necessary to support platform operations during the pilot program and fulfill tenant requirements. Records may be retained longer where necessary to satisfy legal, accounting, tax, fraud-prevention, audit, or record-keeping obligations. Temporary processing states, transient sessions, or unposted documents may be removed according to operational cleanup procedures or upon validated administrative request.

8. User Rights and Deletion Requests

Users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information and registered messaging channels. Deletion requests will be reviewed and processed within a reasonable period, subject to applicable legal, accounting, tax, fraud-prevention, audit, and record-retention obligations. Where appropriate, access to certain historical records may be restricted rather than records being immediately or completely removed.

For detailed instructions on submitting a request, please visit our User Data Deletion Instructions page.
