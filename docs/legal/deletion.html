User Data Deletion Instructions

Standard Operating Procedure for Meta Apps & Platform Integrations | Operator: Muhammad Fikri
Last updated: September 2026

1. Purpose and Overview

In accordance with Meta Platform policies and privacy standards, Muhammad Fikri (operator of the Keuangan-CBL / Financial SaaS pilot platform) provides a clear method for users to request deletion of their personal data, user accounts, and registered WhatsApp intake sender mappings.

Users who interact with the platform through web interfaces or WhatsApp channels may request data removal following the instructions below.

2. How to Submit a Deletion Request

To request data deletion, please complete the following steps:

Step 1: Send a Written Request

Send an email from your registered email address to:
mikaelzo1998@gmail.com

Step 2: Information to Include

Include the following details to help locate and process your records:

  • Subject: User Data Deletion Request - [Your Name / Organization]
  • Identity Details: Your full name and registered user email address (if applicable).
  • WhatsApp Phone Number: If requesting removal of WhatsApp intake mappings or message logs, include your full mobile phone number with country code.
  • Organization: The name of your organization/tenant.
  • Scope of Request: Specify the scope of your request (e.g., removing a WhatsApp sender registration, deleting user login credentials, or removing unposted intake files).
Step 3: Review and Processing

Deletion requests will be reviewed and processed within a reasonable period, subject to applicable legal, accounting, tax, fraud-prevention, audit, and record-retention obligations. You will receive an email confirmation once processing is complete or if further verification is required.

3. Scope and Retention Limitations

We endeavor to delete requested personal information and intake records to the fullest extent possible. However:

  • Sender Mappings & Pending Media: Registered WhatsApp sender mappings can be deactivated, and pending/unprocessed intake files can be deleted upon authorized request.
  • Accounting and Regulatory Records: Certain accounting, audit, tax, fraud-prevention, or legally required records may need to be retained after a deletion request. Where appropriate, access to historical records may be restricted rather than records being immediately deleted.
  • Audit Trails: Traceability logs recording administrative and financial authorizations may be maintained to satisfy auditability and security requirements.

Note on Messaging App Deletion: Deleting a conversation or chat history on your mobile device does not remove previously transmitted intake documents from server storage. To request server-side removal, please submit an email request as described above.
