Privacy Policy

Application: Keuangan-CBL / Financial SaaS | Operator: Muhammad Fikri
Last updated: September 2026

1. Who Operates This Application

This application (Keuangan-CBL / Financial SaaS) is operated by Muhammad Fikri. It is currently used primarily as an internal financial workflow tool during its pilot stage.

2. Information We Process

We only process information needed to handle document intake and internal financial records:

  • WhatsApp phone numbers and messages: Sender phone numbers, text messages, captions, and message timestamps sent to connected intake channels.
  • Uploaded images and PDFs: Document files submitted through web upload or WhatsApp (such as receipts, vendor bills, customer invoices, payment proofs, and project documents).
  • Invoice, receipt, payment, and project document information: Vendor and customer names, dates, line items, monetary amounts, reference numbers, and project codes.
  • OCR and AI extracted information: Text and data fields suggested by automated extraction tools from uploaded documents.

3. Why This Information Is Processed

We process this information to:

  • Receive operational documents from authorized users via web or WhatsApp.
  • Extract readable text and numbers from images and PDFs.
  • Prepare draft financial transaction proposals for review.
  • Record verified entries into company accounting records after human approval.

4. Human Review Queue Requirement

WhatsApp messages and uploaded documents do not automatically create accounting journals. OCR/AI results are proposals that require human review and approval.

Every parsed document and extracted proposal goes into a Review Queue. An authorized team member must verify the details, correct any mistakes, and approve the entry before it affects financial records. Transfer proofs and receipts never turn into expenses automatically.

5. Basic Security and Access Controls

We protect data using practical administrative and technical controls:

  • Access control: Only authorized users can sign in to view or approve records.
  • Organization isolation: Data is kept separated by company/organization.
  • Webhook verification: Inbound WhatsApp messages are verified using secret signatures.
  • Audit logs: We keep records of approvals, edits, and financial actions to maintain clear operational history.

You can request correction or deletion of your personal details, registered phone number, or unprocessed documents at any time.
